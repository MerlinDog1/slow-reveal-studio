# Build status and launch gates

Inspected 27 September 2026 against every numbered section of [README.md](../README.md). [PRODUCT_SPEC.md](PRODUCT_SPEC.md) preserves the full brief. This is a working software prototype, **not a physically validated kit or a live commercial launch**. Example catalogue prices are development fixtures. Generated marketing images are illustrative concepts.

“Implemented” below means code or an artifact exists; it does not imply production service configuration, customer testing or physical approval. “Partial” preserves the remaining scope rather than treating it as completed. External gates need real evidence and an accountable operator. No physical trial, customer result, trade quote or live payment is invented here.

2. **Production agreement:** confirm printer bleed, safe area, registration, white-ink, DPI, outlined-lettering and colour requirements; print exact-size files and measure them. Verify the implemented server-proof acceptance/hash boundary through deployed checkout and RIP output.
3. **Commercial operations:** trade/landed costs, stock, approved tax/shipping/catalogue, support identity, refunds and replacement process. Prototype prices remain indicative.
4. **Services and privacy:** deploy and verify database migration/RLS, private object bucket, webhook signatures/idempotency, email delivery/retries, per-operator access controls, abuse limits, scheduled retention and full retained-data policy. Exercise implemented order-owned sources and legacy-reference protection against configured storage/database services.
5. **Launch presentation:** verify captions, responsive delivery and integration of all fourteen concept assets, then replace them with approved real photography. Verify consent analytics and indexing guards in the deployed site, clear brand/domain rights, publish approved legal/contact content and finish accessibility/mobile/performance review.

Checkout guards should remain disabled until the relevant evidence is recorded. Environment flags are operator assertions, not independent validation of these gates.
