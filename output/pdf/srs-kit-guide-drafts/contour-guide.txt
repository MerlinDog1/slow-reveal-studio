SLOW REVEAL STUDIO - DRAFT FOR PHYSICAL TRIAL
Trace the paths
400 x 500 mm artwork. Guide status: draft-for-physical-trial.

MAKING TRIAL
1. Find one guide path
Start with a short section and follow the printed route. Separate paths remain separate.
2. Trace along the guide
Use the trial marker to follow the path. The finished reference shows the intended line, not a filled region.
3. Keep the open spaces
Do not fill between paths or connect their ends unless the printed guide already connects them.
4. Compare and record
Compare the traced section with the digital reference. Record control, line width and where the guide remains visible.
