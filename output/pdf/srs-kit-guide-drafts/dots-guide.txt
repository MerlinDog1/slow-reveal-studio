SLOW REVEAL STUDIO - DRAFT FOR PHYSICAL TRIAL
Fill the circles
400 x 500 mm artwork. Guide status: draft-for-physical-trial.

MAKING TRIAL
1. Find a small area
Choose a few outlined circles. The spaces between them are part of the image.
2. Fill each circle
Use the trial marker to fill inside each circular boundary. Keep separate circles separate.
3. Follow the changing sizes
Fill the shape that is printed, including the smaller circles. Do not turn every mark into the same size.
4. Compare and record
Compare the filled area with the digital reference. Record guide visibility, control and any marks that join.

DIGITAL COLOUR KEY

Leave unmarked canvas unmarked; it is not an additional numbered colour.
