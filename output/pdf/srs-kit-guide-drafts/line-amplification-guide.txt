SLOW REVEAL STUDIO - DRAFT FOR PHYSICAL TRIAL
Fill the horizontal strips
400 x 500 mm artwork. Guide status: draft-for-physical-trial.

MAKING TRIAL
1. Use a ruler or straight edge
A ruler or straight edge is required for this activity. Align it with the long horizontal edge of a printed strip.
2. Fill the outlined strip
Fill between the two printed boundaries. Follow the changing strip thickness; do not replace it with one thin centre line.
3. Keep the interruptions
Leave blank gaps between strips unmarked. Move the straight edge carefully and observe any dragging or smearing during the trial.
4. Compare and record
Compare with the digital reference. Record ruler handling, edge control and whether the narrow strips are comfortable to fill.
